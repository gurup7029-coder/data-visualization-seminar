📌 What is Data Visualization?

Data Visualization is the graphical representation of information and data. By using visual elements like charts, graphs, and maps, data visualization tools provide an accessible way to see and understand trends, outliers, and patterns in data.

In Python, the primary foundational library is Matplotlib — a multi-platform data visualization library built on NumPy arrays and designed to integrate with SciPy and Pandas.

💡 Real-World Example & Industry Application
🌐 Real-World Example: High-frequency financial trading dashboards visualizing live stock fluctuations and public health pandemic rate tracking.
🏢 Where It Is Used: FinTech quantitative analytics, enterprise executive reporting, healthcare epidemiology, and scientific research publications.

🔑 Key Topics Covered in This Seminar

  • Topic 1: 3D Plotting in Matplotlib: mplot3d toolkit, 3D line & scatter, contour relief, camera angle optimization (view_init), wireframes & surfaces, and surface triangulations.
  • Topic 2: Geographic Data with Basemap: Map projections (cylindrical, pseudo-cylindrical, perspective, conic), physical boundaries, relief backgrounds, and plotting multi-dimensional coordinates.
  • Topic 3: Visualization with Seaborn: High-level statistical exploration, Kernel Density Estimation (KDE), pairplots, faceted grids, factor plots, and time-series bar plots.
